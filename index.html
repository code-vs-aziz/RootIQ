Request deletion of your RXRootIQ data

This page explains how users of the RXRootIQ Android app (developer: Epteck GmbH) can request that their data be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@rxrootiq.com (or info@epteck.com).
  2. Use the subject line RXRootIQ data deletion request.
  3. Include the email address you use to sign in to RXRootIQ, and your full name.
  4. We will verify your identity and process the request. You will receive a confirmation by email.

What we delete

  • Account profile details linked to your Service Provider login (name, email, phone, address where stored).
  • Farmer / field records and visit data you created in the app, where you are the data subject or your organization authorizes deletion.
  • Soil readings, photos, and related files uploaded from your account, subject to the note below.
  • Device identifiers and session data tied to your account on our servers.

What we may keep

  • Data we are legally required to retain (for example tax, billing, or compliance records), for the period required by law.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that can no longer identify you.
  • Backups may retain deleted data for a limited retention window (typically up to 90 days) until they rotate out.
  • If your account was created by an employer or organization, some records may remain under that organization’s control; we will coordinate deletion with them where required.

Timeline

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Complex or organization-managed accounts may take longer; we will tell you if that applies.
